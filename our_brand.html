02 · The logo

Primary logo

The mark is built from navy quarter-circles and blocks, with an amber dot that signals the spark of a good conversation. The stacked lockup is our main logo and should be used wherever space allows.

Always use the master artwork files. Never redraw the logo.

Researchur.Co · Brand identity manual 05

02 · The logo

Anatomy of the mark

Three navy forms: two quarter-circles and a block.

The amber dot sits in the open top-left corner, never on top of navy.

Keep the proportions fixed. The dot is always one quarter of the mark's height.

04 · Clear space and size

Researchur
Consult

Clear space

Keep a margin equal to the height of the mark's navy block (x) on all sides of the logo. Nothing else, including text, images and edges, should enter this space.

Minimum size

Print: 25 mm wide. Digital: 120 px wide. Below this, use the mark only.

Logo misuse

✕Researchur

✕Researchur

✕Researchur

Do not recolour outside the palette.

✕Researchur

  • ✕Do not place the logo on busy photos without clear space.
  • ✕Do not retype the wordmark in another font.
  • ✕Do not rearrange the mark or redraw it.
